## Main listing

App name: **NextJOB**

Suggested category: **Business**

Short description (English):

> Find relevant jobs and prepare a tailored CV from your experience

Short description (Italian):

> Trova offerte pertinenti e prepara un CV su misura dalla tua esperienza

Full description draft:

> NextJOB helps you find relevant roles and prepare an application using the experience in your own CV.
>
> Upload your CV, review the extracted information and choose where you want to work. Explore current job listings, adjust country and work preferences, save roles and prepare a tailored CV with a clear, professional layout.
>
> Start near your chosen city and broaden your search when you need more options. Review each employer's requirements and the source listing before deciding to apply.
>
> You control the information used in your application. Review generated wording and edit your CV before using it. NextJOB does not submit applications automatically or guarantee a job or interview. Employer applications may open on an external website.
>
> CV upload, job matching and document previews do not require a NextJOB account. An internet connection is required. Job availability depends on current listings and your filters.

After paid downloads pass QA, append:

> Document previews are free. Downloading a tailored CV is a separate one-time in-app purchase, with the price displayed before payment. There is no subscription.

Do not claim automatic applications, offline access, guaranteed matches, ATS scores, WhatsApp alerts, working push notifications, or unlimited free document downloads. Confirm that the public service allows guest access before stating no account is required.
